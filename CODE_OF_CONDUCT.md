# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Everyone should be able to participate respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and consider other perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback and accept criticism in good faith.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help resolve disagreements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexualized language and imagery.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, or disruptive advertising.

## Scope

This policy covers project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and the project DingTalk group. It also applies when someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers assess violations in context. Depending on severity, they may remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or impose temporary or permanent bans. Serious violations may warrant immediate restrictions.

Maintainers handle incidents with discretion and respect participants' privacy.
